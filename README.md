# VMG NeXT Construction — Modern Web Platform, Team 360° CMS & AI Blog Studio

Production full-stack replica and engineering upgrade of **vmgnext.com** built with **React 19 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS** for **$0 USD/month** cloud operation.

## 🚀 Key Capabilities

1. **1:1 Visual & Content Replication of `vmgnext.com`:**
   - **Home (`/`)**: Responsive Video Hero, animated Utah Concrete Metrics (`220+ Projects`, `20+ Years`, `100% Satisfaction`), Specialized Services, Featured Projects preview, and Dual Marquee Client Testimonials.
   - **Services (`/services`)**: All 6 active construction divisions (*Tilt-Up, Slabs, Cast-In-Place, Educational Facilities, Commercial Construction, Water Treatment*) + toggleable *High-End Homes* division.
   - **Projects Portfolio (`/projects`)**: All 8 real Utah projects with interactive market filter, concrete budget scopes (`$30K – $5.5M`), square footage (`1,322 – 277,000 SF`), General Contractors, card photo carousels, and full-screen Lightbox gallery.
   - **About Us (`/about-us`)**: Who We Are, Brandon M. Dawson quote card, Mission & Vision ($125M / 360 team members), and 6 Core Values.
   - **Team Directory & Individual Bios (`/team` & `/team/:slug`)**: All 18 active team members (including *Rene Villanueva — Lead Estimator*, *Eduardo Galvan — Assistant Project Manager*, *Sophia Galvan — Marketing Director*, and *Abner Palacios — Director of Special Projects*) + 4 hidden/archived profiles (*Savannah, Zachariah, Levi, Valerie*) toggleable in 1 click.
   - **Careers (`/careers`)**: Inspired by Cardone Ventures with vertical video testimonials (*Valerie, Victor, Scott*), benefits grid, and Google Jobs `JobPosting` schema.
   - **Restoration Division (`/restoration`)**: Filterable 24-photo structural restoration gallery and 3-step disciplined process.
   - **Blog & Insights (`/blog` & `/blog/:slug`)**: Technical construction articles powered by the AI Blog Studio.
   - **Contact Us (`/contact-us`)**: Direct contact cards, inquiry form, and interactive Sandy, Utah office map (`7370 S Creek Rd. STE 204`).

2. **Autonomous `/admin` Control Center:**
   - **AI Blog Studio & Auto-Publisher**: Generate and auto-publish complete SEO construction articles from a single prompt.
   - **Team Members Manager 360°**: Add, edit, reorder, upload/compress card & detail photos, edit biographies, and toggle member visibility (`Visible` / `Hidden`) in 1 click without touching HTML.
   - **Estimating Software API Lead Bridge (`ocv1_...`)**: Connects the *"Request Consultation"* modal directly to VMG NeXT's estimating software API key and logs leads in the built-in CRM.

## 🛠️ Development & Build

npm install
